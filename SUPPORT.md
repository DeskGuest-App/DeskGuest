# Getting support

DeskGuest is a community project, published by one individual. There is no
support department and no support contract or SLA — but help is free, and
every message is read.

## Ways to get help

- **Email:** [support@deskguest.app](mailto:support@deskguest.app) — the
  simplest route for questions, problems, or anything that doesn't fit the
  other channels.
- **Report a bug or share feedback:** the
  [report form on the website](https://deskguest.app/report) files it as an
  issue in this repository for you, with room for screenshots and files.
- **Questions and ideas:**
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DeskGuest-App/DeskGuest/discussions).
- **Guides:** [Help & Support](https://deskguest.app/support) on the website.

## Security problems

Never report a vulnerability in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for
the private reporting route.
