Right here — no account, no leaving the site, nothing to install. Your report goes straight to the people who build DeskGuest, and we read every one. Prefer email? support@deskguest.app reaches the same people.

    What is this about?

    One short line — it becomes the title of your report.

    What is it about?

    If the app showed an uh-oh message, its exact words help most — quote it if you can.

    One step per line helps us retrace it exactly.

    How much does it affect you?
    How were the devices connected? (optional)

    What would it do, and how would it help? A sentence or two is plenty — more is welcome.

    Up to 8 files, 10 MB each, 25 MB together. Screenshots, text and log files, PDFs, ZIPs and short videos. A folder of logs is welcome — please check it holds no passwords or keys first.
