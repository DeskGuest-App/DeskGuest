Every session leaves a receipt and a record on your device, in plain words. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Always visible. Always stoppable.

While anyone is connected — watching or controlling — your device shows it. Help can only happen while you can see it and stop it. That can't be turned off, by anyone, in any setting.

You are always the boss

You choose who may connect and what each helper may do. Helpers you allow can ask to connect — you stay in charge of every session, and you can remove a helper at any time.

One-click stop

STOP ends a session instantly — one click, or the STOP hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F9), with no confirmation step in the way. Ending a session cuts the helper's input first, then closes the connection.

DeskGuest never presses Yes for you

When a job reaches a Windows permission popup — the system's own "allow this change?" prompt — the session waits. That Yes must come from a person at the computer. DeskGuest will never click it through, for any helper, ever. If someone claiming to help you says otherwise, that is a scam signal: stop the session.

Pairing is your act

An AI agent can never pair itself, mint its own code, or widen its own access. Pairing and permission changes are made by the device owner, in person, on the device.

No account. No DeskGuest cloud.

DeskGuest has no account to create and no password to remember. Your identity is a key made on your device — DeskGuest has no copy of it and cannot reset it for you, so your ways back in matter; there are several, and any one is enough.

Connections without a middleman reading along

Some connection paths use a third-party coordination service (for example Tailscale) to help your devices find each other. That service never holds your sessions or your records — your Activity Record stays on your device.

Records stay home

Session records live on the device. Nothing in them is sent anywhere unless you export them.

Reports are yours to send

Reporting from inside the app is in development: when it ships, a Report will include what happened on your device — scrubbed of passwords, keys, and tokens before anything is attached — and nothing will be sent until you tap Send. Today, reports are sent from the form on this website, in your own words.

Cameras are governed, not casual

Where cameras are supported, watching one is written into the Activity Record, sound is off by default, and a workplace policy can turn viewing off entirely.
