Trust & security

DeskGuest is built so the person whose device it is — you — never has to take anyone's word for anything. These are the promises the product is designed to keep, stated plainly so you can hold it to them.

Your agent (AI Agent) is controlling this computer
You see everything · STOP is always here
STOP (ESC)

No hidden access

While anyone is connected — watching or controlling — your device shows it: a named box on every screen, an edge glow, and the helper's own ring-shaped pointer. Help can only happen while you can see it and stop it. That can't be turned off, by anyone, in any setting.

You are always the boss

You choose who may connect and what each helper may do. Helpers you allow can ask to connect — you stay in charge of every session, and you can remove a helper at any time.

One-click stop

STOP ends a session instantly — one click, or the Esc key, with no confirmation step in the way. Ending a session cuts the helper's input first, then closes the connection.

DeskGuest never presses Yes for you

When a job reaches a Windows permission popup — the system's own "allow this change?" prompt — the session waits. That Yes must come from a person at the computer. DeskGuest will never click it through, for any helper, ever. If someone claiming to help you says otherwise, that is a scam signal: stop the session.

No account. No DeskGuest cloud.

DeskGuest has no account to create and no password to remember. Your identity is a recovery key the app makes on your device — DeskGuest has no copy of it and cannot reset it for you, so keep it somewhere safe.

DeskGuest itself runs no cloud. Some connection paths use a third-party coordination service (for example Tailscale) to help your devices find each other. That service never holds your sessions or your records — your Activity Record stays on your device.

Records stay home

Session records live on the device. Nothing in them is sent anywhere unless you export them or send a Report.

Reports are yours to send

A Report includes what happened on your device — and before anything is attached, DeskGuest scans it for passwords, keys, and tokens and removes them. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Cameras are governed, not casual

Where cameras are supported, watching one is written into the Activity Record, sound is off by default, and a workplace policy can turn viewing off entirely.

Scam defense, in plain words

Real DeskGuest help always looks the same. If any of these are missing, stop the session.

1 · You started it

A session begins only when you open DeskGuest and give out a one-time code. DeskGuest never calls, messages, or pops up asking you to let someone in.

2 · You can see them

The helper is named on your screen the whole time, and you see everything they do. No name on screen means no legitimate session.

3 · STOP always works

If STOP or Esc doesn't end it immediately, it isn't DeskGuest behaving as designed — end the session and report it.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately through GitHub's vulnerability reporting for the DeskGuest project: open a private security advisory. Please don't post security problems publicly before they've had a chance to be fixed.
