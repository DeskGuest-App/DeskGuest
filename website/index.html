Rooms, in full

What you see while someone helps

While anyone is connected, your screen shows who is here and what they are doing — and a STOP button that always works. Three things carry that promise, and no setting can switch them off.

The Box

A banner names exactly who is connected — a person or a named AI agent — what they are doing, and carries the STOP button.

The Ghost

Your helper's pointer is a ring carrying their initial, never confused with your own cursor. You watch every move as it happens.

The Glow

A soft edge of light around your screen while a session is live, so a connection is visible even from across the room.

Get help in three simple steps

Step 1

Open DeskGuest

Launch the app on the computer or phone that needs help. No signup, no account.

Step 2

Invite your helper with a code

DeskGuest shows a one-time code on the device. Give it only to someone you trust. It works once, for this pairing, and expires in 10 minutes.

Step 3

Watch & stop anytime

Watch your helper on screen, with you in complete control. Press STOP the moment you want it over.

One product, every kind of help

A person, an AI agent, a whole workplace — the same presence, the same approvals, the same STOP.

Your AI, working beside you

Pair the AI agent you already use. It connects as itself — its own name, its own face — and you set the leash: look only, drive, files, system tasks. It asks before anything sensitive, takes orders as action cards you approve with one tap, and everything it does is written down under its own name.

Quick Fix — help for anyone, in seconds

One invite link opens a session in the browser: voice, chat, screen sharing. One more tap brings in a one-time helper program for full control. When you hang up, it removes itself and the link dies.

Rooms & live collaboration

If you ever experience an issue, a disconnect, or a bug, DeskGuest tells you in plain words what happened and what to do next — and every problem can be reported with a single tap, no complicated forms.
