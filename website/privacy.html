Privacy

DeskGuest's privacy design is short enough to state in full: no account, no DeskGuest cloud, no telemetry — and records that live on your device.

The whole policy, in three lines

No account

There is nothing to sign up for and no profile of you anywhere. Your identity is a key made on your device; DeskGuest has no copy and cannot reset it.

No DeskGuest cloud

DeskGuest runs no servers that hold your sessions, your records, or your files. Devices connect to each other; optional relays carry sealed sessions they cannot read.

No telemetry

The app sends nothing home. There is no analytics, no usage reporting, and no crash upload. If something goes wrong, you decide whether to send a Report — and you are the sender, every time.

Your Activity Record stays home

The record of who connected, what was approved, and what changed lives on the device itself. It leaves only when you export it or attach it to a Report you choose to send.

Reports are scrubbed before they're sent

Before anything is attached to a Report, DeskGuest scans it for passwords, keys, and tokens and removes them. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Coordination services see almost nothing

Some connection paths use a third-party coordination service (for example Tailscale) so devices can find each other. It never holds your sessions or your records — discovery only.

Your devices sync sealed

Your own devices share your roster, groups, profile, and settings over the same sealed connections. An optional always-available copy lives only on a hub you own, sealed so the hub can't read it.

This website practices what the product preaches: it sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers — every file, font, and image is served from this site itself. If that ever changes, this page changes first, in plain words.
