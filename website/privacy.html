Privacy

DeskGuest's privacy design comes down to three rules: no account, no DeskGuest cloud, no telemetry. Your records stay on your device.

DeskGuest is pre-release: v0.0.1 is a release candidate, not yet publicly released. This page describes how that build behaves.

The three rules

No account

There is nothing to sign up for and no profile of you anywhere. Your identity is a key made on your device; DeskGuest has no copy and cannot reset it.

No DeskGuest cloud

DeskGuest runs no servers that hold your sessions, your records, or your files. Devices connect to each other; optional relays carry sealed sessions they cannot read.

No telemetry

The app sends nothing home. There is no analytics, no usage reporting, and no crash upload. If something goes wrong, whether to report it is always your decision — today reports are sent from the form on this website, by you, every time.

What that means in practice

Your Activity Record stays on your device

The record of who connected, what was approved, and what changed lives on the device itself. It leaves only when you export it.

Reports are always your choice

Today you send reports yourself through the report form on this website. The app never sends them on its own. Reporting from inside the app is in development, and it will send nothing until you choose Send.

What a coordination service sees

If you choose a connection path that uses Tailscale, Tailscale sees which of your devices are online and their network addresses so they can find each other. It never carries or stores your sessions or records.

Your devices sync sealed

Your own devices share your roster, groups, profile, and settings over the same sealed connections. An optional always-available copy lives only on a hub you own, sealed so the hub can't read it.

What reaches us

Reports you send through the report form are posted on DeskGuest's public issue tracker, with whatever you put in them. Email you send to support@deskguest.app reaches us directly, and we use it only to answer you. Purchases and donations, once the store opens, go through Gumroad (deskguest.gumroad.com), which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. DeskGuest never sees your card.

This website follows the same rules: it sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from other servers. Every file, font and image comes from deskguest.app. If that ever changes, this page changes first, in plain words.
