What is being protected

Your device and everything on it; the screen and input during a session; your identity key and your ways back in; and the Activity Record that records what happened.

Who it is protected against

The fake helper

A scammer who phones or messages claiming to be support and asks you to "let them in." Defense: help can only begin when you start it — you open DeskGuest and hand out a one-time code yourself. Quick Fix works the same way: attended only, one-time code, nothing installed. Either way the helper is named on your screen the whole time, and DeskGuest never presses Yes on system prompts. Is this a scam?

A helper who oversteps

A paired person or AI agent doing more than you allowed. Defense: per-helper permission levels set by you; sensitive actions arrive as approval cards before they run; presence that cannot be hidden; STOP that cuts input first; and an Activity Record that can't be quietly edited.

Someone watching the wire

An observer on the network, or a relay carrying your session. Defense: sessions are end-to-end encrypted between the two devices' own keypairs, and there is no DeskGuest server in the middle able to read, log, or hand over a session. The exact protocol and any relay path are documented in the public source.

A lost or stolen device

Defense: your identity is a key held on the device, and any surviving device of yours can strike a lost device's key from your circle. Recovery doors (a sealed file, a trusted person, split pieces, a word sheet) restore you — never a central reset DeskGuest controls.

The consent floor

Four properties are the product, not settings — no policy, admin, or helper can switch them off: the connected helper is always named on your screen; STOP always works; DeskGuest refuses to type credentials and never clicks through system permission prompts; and the Activity Record cannot be quietly edited.

STOP is the emergency halt: press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F9 or the on-screen STOP button. Moving your mouse or typing does not take control back from a helper. Control changes hands only through Give control and Take control.

Never, by design

  • ✓No telemetry or analytics — the build contains no analytics code to switch off.
  • ✓No credential is ever readable back — under any approval, by any interface.
  • ✓No mining your messages, call logs, photos, or clipboard history to build context.
  • ✓No network interception, packet capture, or traffic manipulation tooling.
  • ✓No silent permission grants — access is given only when the owner approves it.
  • ✓No API keys or paid AI usage bundled in — any AI agent uses your own account and key.
  • ✓No fake capability — a thing a device cannot do reports unavailable, with a reason.

What DeskGuest does not claim

Stated plainly, so nobody has to guess:

It can't judge your trust for you

If you hand a code to the wrong person, DeskGuest shows you who is connected and stops them the instant you say so — but the choice of who to trust is yours. The scam page exists to help with exactly that.

It doesn't protect a device that's already compromised

If malicious software already runs on a device with full rights, no remote-help tool can make that device safe. On a compromised device, DeskGuest's own Activity Record can't be trusted either.

Approved actions are the approver's

When you tap Approve on an action card, DeskGuest runs it and records your approval. Read cards before approving them — that's what they're for.

It isn't released yet

DeskGuest is pre-release: v0.0.1 is a release candidate and the public installer is not final. Everything on this page describes how the software is designed and how it behaves in our own testing on Windows 11 (the V1 platform), with the Android companion tested on a Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. The IT page lists the support matrix.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, or email support@deskguest.app with 'Security report' in the subject. Please don't post security problems publicly before they've had a chance to be fixed.
