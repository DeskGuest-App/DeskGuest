Secure, direct connections for workplaces

DeskGuest lets your own people — and the AI agents you choose — help across your devices, under rules you set and records you keep.

Direct connections, no DeskGuest cloud

Devices connect to each other; there is no DeskGuest server in the middle holding your sessions. DeskGuest itself runs no cloud. Some connection paths use a third-party coordination service (for example Tailscale) to help your devices find each other — that service never holds your sessions or your records.

Local audit logs

Every session is written into the Activity Record on the device itself: who connected, what was approved, what changed. Records export locally (including CSV) for your own review — no cloud telemetry.

Groups with plain-language rules

Every group has an admin. Admins control membership and the structural acts — inviting people is admin-only. Members can access and enroll devices. Removing a person shows you the full blast list first — every device and group they could reach — before anything is revoked, and the last admin can never be removed.

Effective Policy: see what actually applies

Rules from your workplace + choices on the device = what actually applies — DeskGuest shows you the result, not just the rules. Every feature carries a policy control, including camera viewing, patch approvals, and automatic fixes. The things that protect the device owner — being seen while helping, STOP, the uneditable record — are always true and are never policy settings.

Group rules, in plain language

  • ✓Allow AI view only
  • ✓Require Host approve on mouse move
  • ✓Revoke at idle

Three of the rules a group admin can set, written the way the group sees them. The full rule set — and what actually applies on each device — is on the Effective Policy card above.

Deploy to your team: one person runs the hub once for the workplace; sites and devices enroll with a code. Personal and workplace scopes live in one app, with personal scope walled off completely.
