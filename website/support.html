Uh-oh! Still stuck? Report an issue.

Our promise: when something goes wrong, DeskGuest never fails quietly. You get a plain account of what happened, what it means, and the one thing to do next — at the moment it happens, not buried in a log.

A report sent from this website is your own words, plus any files you choose to attach. Reporting from inside the app is in development: when it arrives, the app will build the report for you from what happened on this device — scrubbed of passwords, keys, and tokens before anything is attached — and nothing will be sent until you tap Send. We read every report; fixes arrive in updates.

Reporting from the app — in development

When in-app reporting arrives, the diagnostics will travel with your report — scrubbed of secrets before anything is attached, and nothing sent until you tap Send. Until then, the form at the top of this page reaches the same place, whether the app is running or won't open at all, with no account needed.

What a good report includes

  • What you were trying to do, in your own words
  • What happened instead — the uh-oh message says it plainly; quote it if you can
  • Whether STOP worked (it should — tell us if it didn't)
  • Your device type: a Windows 11 computer, or your Android phone model

Never include passwords, recovery keys, or pairing codes in a report.
