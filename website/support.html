Uh-oh! Still stuck? Report an issue.

Our promise: when something goes wrong, DeskGuest never fails quietly. You get a plain account of what happened, what it means, and the one thing to do next — at the moment it happens, not buried in a log.

A Report is a diagnostics package from your device: what happened, in order. Before anything is attached, DeskGuest scans it for passwords, keys, and tokens and removes them. Nothing is sent until you tap Send — you are the sender, every time.

We read every report; fixes ship in updates.

Get the app to send a report

Reports are sent from inside the app, because that's where the diagnostics live. If the app won't open at all, you can also ask for help on the DeskGuest discussions on GitHub or open an issue in the issue tracker.

What a good report includes

  • What you were trying to do, in your own words
  • What happened instead — the uh-oh message says it plainly; quote it if you can
  • Whether STOP worked (it should — tell us if it didn't)
  • Your device type: a Windows 11 computer, or your Android phone model

Never include passwords, recovery keys, or pairing codes in a report — DeskGuest removes secrets from its own diagnostics, and your words deserve the same care.
