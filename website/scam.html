Scam safety

DeskGuest will never call you and never ask for a code by phone.

How to tell real DeskGuest help from a tech-support scam, and what to do if you're not sure.

DeskGuest is pre-release. v0.0.1 is a release candidate and isn't publicly released yet. This page describes how it is designed to behave.

How DeskGuest keeps you safe

See Their Name Before Anything Starts

AAlice (Support)✓

The person or AI agent helping you is named on your screen for the whole session, and you see everything they do.

One-Click STOP and Take Control

STOP

One click on STOP, or the hotkey Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F9, ends the session right away. Moving your mouse or typing doesn't take control back. Press Take control when you want it back.

Zero Standing Access After Quick Fix Closes

Session Ended
Nothing installed. Nothing left behind.

Quick Fix installs nothing and leaves nothing behind. When the session ends, the one-time code stops working.

The three signs of the real thing

1 · You started it

Help begins only when you start it. With Quick Fix, you open it yourself and read a one-time code to someone you already know. For a regular helper, you pair their device by hand, once. DeskGuest never calls, messages, or pops up asking you to let someone in.

2 · You can see them

The helper is named on your screen the whole time, and you see everything they do. No name on screen means no legitimate session.

3 · STOP always works

If STOP doesn't end it immediately, it isn't DeskGuest behaving as designed — end the session and report it.

Red flags — hang up and close it

  • !Someone contacted you first, claiming your computer has a problem
  • !You're asked to install something that isn't from this site or the project's GitHub
  • !You're told to keep the session secret, or to hurry
  • !You're asked for a password, a bank login, or a recovery key. A real helper never needs them.
  • !You're told someone else will click Yes on a permission popup for you.
  • !There's no name on your screen, or STOP doesn't end it instantly

Any one of these is enough. Ending a session costs nothing and can always be restarted with someone you trust.

If it already happened

Press STOP or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F9. If the session won't end, close DeskGuest. If it still won't end, shut the computer down. When it's back on, remove that helper from your paired list in DeskGuest, then send us a report.

The one-sentence test

Real help starts with you, stays visible to you, and stops the moment you say so. Anything else is not DeskGuest — whatever the person on the phone claims.
