Presence: seeing who is connected

While a helper is connected, their name is always on your screen and everything they do is visible. Moving your mouse doesn't take control back; use Take control, or STOP (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F9) to end the session at once.

Presence & consent

Quick Fix

Quick Fix is for one-off help while you're at the computer. You give your helper a one-time code. Nothing is installed and nothing is left behind when the session ends.

Rooms & meetings

A session is help on one device. Several helpers, people or AI agents, can join it, but only one has control at a time, and control moves only when someone uses Give control or Take control. A room is a standing meeting space where people gather. If it's always just you and one device, you don't need a room.
