Rooms & meetings

Fixing a machine? Start a session — several helpers, people or AIs, can join it, take turns, or drive side by side. Gathering people in a standing meeting place? Host a room. A session is help happening on one device; a room is where people gather to give that help together. If it's always just you and one device, you may never need a room — and that's fine.
